
# MTG Color Code Symbols & Names

## Basic Colors

- W – White
- U – Blue
- B – Black
- R – Red
- G – Green


## Two-Color Combinations (Guilds)

- WU – Azorius
- UB – Dimir
- BR – Rakdos
- RG – Gruul
- GW – Selesnya
- WB – Orzhov
- UR – Izzet
- BG – Golgari
- RW – Boros
- GU – Simic


## Three-Color Combinations (Shards and Wedges)

- WUG / Bant – Bant
- UWB / Esper – Esper
- BRU / Grixis – Grixis
- RBG / Jund – Jund
- GRW / Naya – Naya
- WBG / Abzan – Abzan
- URW / Jeskai – Jeskai
- BGU / Sultai – Sultai
- RWB / Mardu – Mardu

## Four-Color Combinations (No-Color Names)

- Glint (No White / UBGR)
- Dune (No Blue / WBRG)
- Ink (No Black / WURG)
- Witch (No Red / WUBG)
- Yore (No Green / WUBR)
